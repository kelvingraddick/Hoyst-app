# Hoyst Design Direction

The canonical Hoyst design system now lives in `design-guide/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`.

The 2026 restyle target is based on the tracked references in `design-guide/hoyst-restyle-2026/`. The app should use dual light and dark fidelity, frosted glass surfaces, spectrum rings, larger labeled navigation, and spacing normalized around a 390 to 393 pt mobile baseline.

Core product direction:

- Momentum is a personal reliability score, not an activity score.
- Momentum equals completed available opportunities divided by available opportunities, multiplied by 100.
- Circle Progression is separate and measures the group showing up together.
- Bottom tabs are Home, Circles, Tap In, Momentum, and Profile.
- Explore discovery folds into Circles. Inbox behavior folds into Home notifications and companion update surfaces.
