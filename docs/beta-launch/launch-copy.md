## 35–45 second demo script

0–6s, camera: “What’s one thing you keep telling yourself you’re going to stick with? I built Hoyst to help with that.”
6–17s, screen recording: “Pick a commitment and choose how often you want to follow through. Start with something realistic.” Show creating a simple daily or weekly commitment with demonstration content.
17–27s: “Tap In to record how it went, and see your progress.” Show one Tap In.
27–35s: “You can do it on your own, or bring a friend into a Circle.” Show the invite action using a demo Circle, without exposing private activity.
35–45s, camera: “The free iPhone beta is open. Try one commitment for two weeks and tell me what helps or gets in the way. Link in my bio.” Overlay: US • 18+ • iPhone.

Record on a real installed build. Use demo data, captions, 9:16 video and no private messages. Export one clean master, then upload separately to each platform.

For a quick first recording, use one example throughout, such as walking three times a week. Capture four short clips: the Commitment, recording a Tap In, progress, and the Circle invitation action. Add the script as voiceover if recording yourself on camera would slow the first version down. Do not wait for a polished production before testing the message.

## Circle walkthrough, during the first week

Want someone in your corner while you work on a commitment?

Create a Circle in Hoyst and share its invitation with a friend.

If they’re new: install Hoyst through TestFlight, return to the invitation page, then tap Open Hoyst.

Start with a shared goal and a pace that works for both of you. The free iPhone beta is open to US adults 18+.

## Feedback questions, during weeks one and two

Post one at a time: “What stopped you getting started?” / “What made you return?” / “What felt confusing?”

For two short tester conversations: ask what they hoped Hoyst would help with, have them describe their first experience, ask about the last time they returned, and ask what nearly made them stop. Get permission before quoting or sharing their story.
