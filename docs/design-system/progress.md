## Your stats and retained activity

The approved stats source is `exec-29009a9e-bb04-4fb8-9869-423cb58f61ea.png` in the task's generated-image directory. The selected-day card is directly beneath the calendar and shows two entries with category artwork, quantity/status descriptions, status icons, dividers, and independent detail targets. View all activity opens the complete paginated day list. Details are read-only and include retained notes/photos and original schedule-period metadata.

The calendar uses Sunday-first aligned columns, circular Home-style markers and smooth dashed paths that extend to the viewport edges on complete weeks without visible turns between rows. Success stays green inside today's blue outline; another selected day has a thinner outline. Partial entries use a blue badge, protection an orange shield, and days without successful/partial/protected coverage remain neutral. At accessibility text sizes the decorative connectors disappear. Calendar cells retain 44/48-point minimum vertical targets, with hit slop; text and activity rows grow naturally. New action labels use the local theme's accessible blue in dark mode.

The member's saved timezone governs initial month/today. Earlier months select their last day, future months/days are disabled, and a midnight tick refreshes history and advances a default today selection while preserving a separately selected earlier day. Successful stored entries count once each; Active days counts unique success dates. Partial, failed, skipped and restored entries are excluded from actual totals. Personal bests reuse the existing profile summary. The protection explanation distinguishes continuity from actual Tap Ins.

Read-only callables `getProgressMonth` and `getProgressDayActivity` are deployed in `us-central1` on `hoyst-firebase-app`. Authentication fixes the owner; caller-supplied foreign owner fields are rejected. The latter returns 20 entries per page with full timestamp precision and codepoint-ordered stable identifiers. Cursors are bound to owner, date and timezone. Private Circle metadata after leaving is resolved from retained personal metadata rather than exposing current private labels. Misses require a stored expired expected opportunity plus historical membership evidence. Restores retain their original effective day and period. No historical rows, XP, balances or grants are written by either endpoint.

Compatibility currently reads retained owner check-ins, opportunities and past-commitment metadata in bounded 500-document pages before deriving the requested month/day. Total read cost therefore grows with retained history. This avoids losing legacy entries without indexed timestamps or writing a migration. The existing owner UID collection-group index is sufficient; live requests succeeded without new index or rule changes. Client caches include account, timezone and month/date, refresh on focus/foreground and relevant private activity changes, and remove the departing account's history cache. Empty, loading, retry, missing-activity and unavailable-photo states are explicit.
