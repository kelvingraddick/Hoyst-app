A few good questions.

What is an accountability Circle?

A Circle is a group of people following through on a shared Commitment. You can see each other’s Tap Ins, notice progress, and send a helpful nudge when someone needs one.

Can I bring my friends?

Yes. Create a Circle and share its invitation link. Circle access can be open, invite only, or require approval.

Do I have to join a Circle?

You can also keep personal Commitments. Circles add direct support from other people when you want it.

Is Hoyst available on Android?

The public beta is currently for iPhone. Join the updates list above for news about future platform availability.

How do I share feedback?

In Hoyst, open Settings and choose Get help / Report a problem or Request a feature. You can also use TestFlight feedback, email Hoyst, or message @kg.codes.
